Can the tenant make alterations to the property?

psychology

1. Direct Answer

Yes, subject to conditions. The tenant may make non-structural alterations, provided they obtain prior written consent from the landlord. Structural alterations are strictly prohibited. article Page 4 • Clause 2.c

2. What the document says

"The Tenant shall not make any structural alterations or additions to the Demised Premises without the express prior written consent of the Landlord, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld for non-structural interior modifications."

3. Legal Perspective

This is a standard "qualified covenant" against alterations. The phrase "shall not be unreasonably withheld" provides the tenant with some statutory protection under landlord-tenant law, meaning the landlord cannot arbitrarily refuse minor cosmetic changes, provided proper process is followed.

4. Practical Meaning

You can paint walls, install removable shelving, or change carpets, but you must ask the landlord first in writing. You cannot knock down walls, change plumbing, or alter electrical wiring.

lightbulb

5. Recommendation

Before undertaking any work, draft a formal request outlining the proposed non-structural changes. Wait for explicit written approval to avoid breach of lease conditions.
